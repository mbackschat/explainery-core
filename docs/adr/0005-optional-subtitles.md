# 0005 Optional subtitle sidecars

Accepted 2026-10-16.

## Decision

Explainery provides `subtitles [project-dir] [--audio <file>] [--transcript <file>] [--out <prefix>]` for every story. It emits SRT and WebVTT from one set of cues, using the full-film narration clock even when a scene is focused. The default prefix is `renders/<story-id>-full`, relative to the current working directory, matching full-film rendering. Explicit scene-range flags are rejected.

[story.yaml's narration](../../src/narration.ts) owns readable words. Recorded word timestamps own synchronization. The [shared alignment](../../src/align.ts) matches recognition to the script, including whitespace splits and merges, and corrects drift into detected pauses. Unmatched words are interpolated, so alignment and phrase boundaries require review. TTS pronunciation substitutions never enter subtitles. Existing scene timing is not regenerated by this command.

The [subtitle formatter](../../src/subtitles.ts) groups words at composition boundaries, sentence endings, long pauses and size limits. Cues contain at most two lines of 42 characters and last at most six seconds. These are editing aids, not proof that a language's phrases or reading speed are appropriate. Subtitles remain optional files; this decision does not add a player control, burn text into pictures or embed tracks in MP4s.

## Consequences

Stories can distribute subtitles without rerendering video or changing composition layout. Both output formats share text and cue timings. A retained, reviewed transcript permits reproducible generation; a replaced narration requires fresh alignment and review. Regeneration replaces the destination sidecars, including manual edits.

Core owns the exporter and its tests. Stories own transcripts and subtitle deliveries. No story-specific names, pronunciation exceptions or media belong in the framework. [Tests](../../tests/subtitles.test.ts) guard text fidelity, lexical splits and merges, cue limits, pauses, serialization and CLI side effects.
